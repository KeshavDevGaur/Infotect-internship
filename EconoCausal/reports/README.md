## Day 4 Key Findings

The Day 4 analysis investigated:

1. The distribution of `redemption_status`.
2. Redemption and non-redemption counts.
3. Observed redemption rates across campaigns.
4. Redemption rates across campaign types.
5. The top 10 campaigns by observed redemption rate.

Campaign-level rates should be interpreted alongside the number of records in each campaign. Rates calculated from small samples may be less reliable.

## Reproducibility

The CSV files are generated by the corresponding Jupyter notebooks in the `../notebooks/` directory.

Original raw datasets are maintained locally and are not stored in this repository.

## Important Limitation

These reports describe data quality and observed redemption patterns. They do not establish that campaigns or discounts caused changes in purchasing behavior.

Any future causal analysis must define the treatment, outcome, covariates, and identification assumptions appropriately.
